METHOD AND LIMITATIONS
Timestamps come from local saved conversation records. Git history cross-checks the document and renames, but commit spacing and file birth/modification times are not used to measure study duration.
A response window runs from the actual user message to completion of the assistant reply. Aborted or replayed requests and subagent conversations are excluded from the exchange counts.
A reconstructed working window joins consecutive completed exchanges when the gap from the previous reply completion to the next user message is at most 30 minutes. Longer gaps are omitted entirely; windows are not padded before or after an exchange.
The window total includes assistant processing, intervening reading/writing, possible idle time, diagrams, formatting and compilation. It is an accounting convention, NOT measured human study time or a reliable lower/upper bound.
Silent independent reading, notes written outside these exchanges, longer study intervals between messages, and other conversations can be missing. The selected July discussions are related earlier work, not the continuous October rereading.
